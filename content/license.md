## Diagrams and written notes

The architecture diagrams on this site, and the notes that accompany them, are
my own work. I drew every diagram from scratch for my master's thesis; they are
rendered here directly from their original TikZ source.

**You may** share and adapt them — in a lecture, a blog post, a paper, a thesis —
provided you give credit and indicate whether you changed anything.

**You may not** use them for commercial purposes without asking me first. If you
want to, [email me](mailto:abhishek.nannuri@outlook.com); I am usually happy to
say yes.

### How to cite

> Nannuri, A. (2026). *Vision-Based Dense Rewards for Finetuning Manipulation
> Policies via Reinforcement Learning* [Master's thesis]. Hochschule
> Schmalkalden.

BibTeX:

```bibtex
@mastersthesis{nannuri2026rewards,
  author = {Nannuri, Abhishek},
  title  = {Vision-Based Dense Rewards for Finetuning Manipulation
            Policies via Reinforcement Learning},
  school = {Hochschule Schmalkalden},
  year   = {2026}
}
```

If you are reusing a single figure, crediting "Abhishek Nannuri, CC BY-NC 4.0"
with a link back to this site is enough.
